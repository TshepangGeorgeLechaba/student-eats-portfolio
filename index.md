## 📌 Project Overview
**Student Eats** is an Agile digital marketplace platform designed to connect CPUT residence food vendors with student buyers. The platform facilitates affordable meal ordering, real-time tracking, and residence delivery. 

This digital portfolio serves as a comprehensive **Portfolio of Evidence (PoE)** documenting our team's Agile workflows, social contracts, backlog prioritizing, sprint execution, and retrospectives.
